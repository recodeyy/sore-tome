# SERO AI 12-Month Roadmap

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Author:** Chief AI Product Officer

This document details the rollout sequence for AI capabilities over the next 12 months.

## 1. Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation & Quick Wins (Months 1–3)
- **Features:** Society Pulse Dashboard, Complaint Clustering, and Financial Anomaly Radar.
- **Dependencies:** PostgreSQL PGVector setup, Knex migration execution.
- **Deliverable:** Fully connected screens on live backend API data.

### Phase 2: Predictive Maintenance (Months 4–6)
- **Features:** Failure risk scoring, work order auto-scheduling suggestion.
- **Dependencies:** Historical maintenance logs dataset collection.

### Phase 3: Bounded Autopilot (Months 7–9)
- **Features:** Outbox event-driven multi-step orchestration suggestions.
- **Dependencies:** BullMQ workflow worker deployment.

### Phase 4: Scenario Simulator (Months 10–12)
- **Features:** What-if policy impact sandbox.
- **Dependencies:** Analytical datamart view creation.
