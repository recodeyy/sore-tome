# AI Feature Gap Analysis

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Analyst:** Chief AI Product Officer

This document analyzes the gap between the currently implemented AI features in SERO and the desired target state of a fully proactive, autonomous-yet-safe society management platform.

## 1. Current State vs. Target State Gaps

| Capability Area | Current Implementation | Target Innovation State | Critical Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Predictive Operations** | Heuristic rules for complaints & maintenance risk scores. | True multi-variable forecasting for visitor/gate traffic, parcel volume, and cash-flow delays. | Lack of predictive time-series models for demand forecasting. |
| **Operational Autopilot** | Static recommendations in Society Pulse dashboard. | Bounded, multi-step orchestration (e.g., auto-draft notice, assign work order, notify residents on confirmation). | Suggestions are single-step and lack workflow orchestration. |
| **Digital Twin** | None. | What-if simulation engine for fee changes, shift schedules, and parking policy adjustments. | Absence of simulation sandbox and scenario modeling. |
| **Emergency Coordination** | Standard SOS notifications. | Hyperlocal emergency routing, building-zone panic aggregation, and automated responder checklists. | SOS triggers are reactive broadcasts; lacks intelligent routing and zone-level clustering. |
| **Governance Support** | Standard voting and agenda UI. | Meeting agenda auto-generation from complaint trends, resolution tracking, and compliance checklists. | Minutes and agendas must be compiled manually by the Admin. |

## 2. Priority Gaps to Fill
1. **Scenario Planning (Digital Twin):** Committee members need to model the financial impact of maintenance hikes before publishing bills.
2. **Hyperlocal Panic/SOS Aggregation:** Guards need immediate spatial clustering of emergency signals to detect building-wide hazards (e.g., fire on 5th floor).
3. **Smart Collections / Payment Assistance:** Automated household coordination and individualized installment proposals.
