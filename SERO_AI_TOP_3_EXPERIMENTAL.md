# SERO AI Top 3 Experimental Bets

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Author:** Chief AI Product Officer

These high-risk, high-reward features could redefine the category if successful.

---

## 1. Policy Impact Simulator (Digital Twin)
- **Target User:** Secretary / Committee.
- **Why Now:** Changing society bylaws often causes resident pushback. Simulator models this impact.
- **Experimental Risk:** Complex heuristic assumptions about human behavior could yield inaccurate simulations.

## 2. Hyperlocal SOS Aggregator
- **Target User:** Security Manager / Guard.
- **Why Now:** In emergencies, every second counts. Zone-based alarm clustering helps security locate fires or break-ins.
- **Experimental Risk:** False alarms could desensitize responders.

## 3. Community Interest Matchmaker
- **Target User:** Resident.
- **Why Now:** Builds community bonding within the society.
- **Experimental Risk:** High privacy hurdles. Requires strict resident consent and opt-ins.
