# SERO AI Top 3 Strategic Differentiators

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Author:** Chief AI Product Officer

These features take 3–9 months to build but create a long-term data moat.

---

## 1. Predictive Maintenance Risk Engine
- **Target User:** Facility Manager / Committee.
- **Why Now:** Repairs are SERO's highest operational expense. Predictive maintenance cuts costs by 20%.
- **Data Moat:** Cumulative historical logs of machine failures build an asset degradation profile unique to SERO.

## 2. Privacy-Safe Issue Radar
- **Target User:** Committee Member.
- **Why Now:** Provides a high-level view of resident concerns without infringing on privacy.
- **Data Moat:** Multi-channel aggregation across notices, votes, and complaints.

## 3. Smart Collections Assistant
- **Target User:** Treasurer.
- **Why Now:** Collection delays impact society operations.
- **Data Moat:** Personalized reminder modeling based on unit payment behavior history.
